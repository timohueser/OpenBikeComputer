Ride Assistant · firmware study

Unmodified 240×320 PNGs from the actual firmware screens in obc-sim. Fonts, colours, maps and button handling come from the device code.

Try it: ./run.sh opens the four-result comparison on the Grimsel GPX. Left/Right arrows select a result; Enter previews it. The Controls panel has a candidate set selector, a result selector and Jump to stage. Arrive at shop and Rejoin original route supply simulated location events.

Other areas: ./run.sh /absolute/path/to/map.obcm creates a local study. Use --center LON,LAT in microdegrees to place it, or --assistant-route /absolute/path/to/ride.gpx. A coastal map's geometric centre can lie at sea.

Provisional selection: keep shops within 400 m extra for a complete visit. A detour must reach a shop at least 500 m sooner, or save at least 50 m climbing at no greater distance. Exclude it if an on-route shop is no farther and no harder. Show at most four, with space for a useful detour when available. No combined effort score or forced filler.

Study data: fictional shops and access paths, prepared route legs, English and metric. These paths can cross unrideable terrain. Real POI search, access routing and GPS arrival detection remain outside this prototype.
