    Built for the long way round

    The things that matter when you're a long way from the nearest plug or signal.

    Sunlight-readable display

    A memory-LCD (MIP) screen that's crisp and always on — and only redraws what changes, so it stays readable in direct sun without burning power.

    Lasts for days

    The low-power display and microcontroller sip from the battery — so you measure runtime in days on the trail, not hours between charges.

    3D-printed case

    Open hardware: the enclosure is 3D-printable, so you can make it, mount it your way, mod it, and repair it yourself — no proprietary parts.

    Open-source software

    Every part of it is open — firmware and the tools that feed it. Read it, build it, change what you don't like, and make it your own.

    Style your own maps

    Pick a region and style the map to your taste, then build it yourself. Your colours, your detail — the map looks the way you want it to.

    Completely cloud-free

    No account, no subscription, no cloud. Your maps and your rides live on the device and on your computer — and only there. Your data stays yours.
