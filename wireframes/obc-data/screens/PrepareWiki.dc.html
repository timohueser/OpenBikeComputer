Prepare a new wiki version

One long job builds a version from the Wikimedia dumps and publishes it to R2. Every bake after that reads the version and makes no Wikimedia requests.

Inputs

Wikidata JSON dump2026-11-25142 GB
Wikipedia Enterprise HTML2026-11-2061 GB✓ token
Wikipedia SQL tables2026-11-209 GB
Commons photosAPI, only for selected subjects, 4 requests/s
Article languages
Subjects · every OSM object with a wikidata tag, on the whole planet: about 2.9 million. One version serves every target.

When it finishes

wiki 2026-11-25 shows in Sources. In every target that uses an older version, these layers become stale: device/landmarks, planner/search, and what follows them (device/catalog, planner/places, planner/index). Closing this page does not stop the job. A stopped job keeps its downloads and resumes.
