Strong

  • At home on the website: it is the planner riders already know. Points panel, whole-trip profile with the days on one line, layers, versions and export in the bar.
  • On the road: the map is the screen, and the options and the amber Send to device are in thumb reach.
  • The panel footer keeps the preset and the trip figures under any answer, so the rider never loses the route.

Weak

  • The box is at the top of the phone screen, far from the thumb. A second query needs two hands.
  • Answers replace the 360 px panel, so comparing a result with the day list means going back and forth.
  • In the tent the answer is a sheet over most of the map. The profile is secondary, so there is no natural place for the day window or for dragging a day end.
