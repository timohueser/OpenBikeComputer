P3 · Map, strip, tool bar

  • Two fixed pieces at the bottom: a tool bar (Search, Days, More) and one dock above it that holds one thing at a time: the profile strip at rest, the compact answer, or the days. The map keeps 559 of 844 pt at rest.
  • Tent job: four screens, never more than the map and a list together; the list keeps three rows visible with a picker open.
  • Road job: the options are rows named by what they win; the selected one carries “Use this route”.
  • Weak: editing a day end takes three moves (Days, the day, drag the strip up). The strip and an open answer never show together, so the answer ticks on the profile disappear. The permanent tool bar costs 83 pt.
  • Landscape shows the whole trip’s profile with every day end as a handle; in this direction it is the best surface for editing days.
