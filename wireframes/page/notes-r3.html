P1 · One sheet

  • The detent is the switch. Low: the box and one line. Medium: the profile, or the compact answer. High: the days, or the full answer. Focusing the box takes the sheet to the top and hides the map.
  • The one line under the box changes with the moment: the trip totals, the scrubber reading on the profile, or the answer count. So the low detent is always useful.
  • The picker opens under its chip and the sheet grows by exactly its height, so the list and the markers stay in view.
  • Weak: while an answer is open, the medium detent holds the answer, so the profile is one step away. On the profile only the selected day’s end is a drag handle; the other day ends are dots.

P2 · Three views

  • A two-row navigation bar: Back, the plan name, search, Undo and “···”, then Map | Profile | Days. Each segment is one full-screen view.
  • At rest the box is docked at the bottom of the Map view, in thumb reach. An answer replaces the box in the same dock: chips, a count, the results, and one action while a row is selected.
  • Editing days: the clearest profile of the three (full width, low on the screen), and the days as one pure list.
  • Weak: the two-row bar costs 150 pt in every view, and the segments sit at the top, out of thumb reach. A list never shows next to the map, so you switch views more often. For a one-day route the segments add little.

P3 · Map, strip, tool bar

  • Two fixed pieces at the bottom: a tool bar (Search, Days, More) and one dock above it that holds one thing at a time: the profile strip at rest, the compact answer, or the days. The map keeps 559 of 844 pt at rest.
  • Tent job: four screens, never more than the map and a list together; the list keeps three rows visible with a picker open.
  • Road job: the options are rows named by what they win; the selected one carries “Use this route”.
  • Weak: editing a day end takes three moves (Days, the day, drag the strip up). The strip and an open answer never show together, so the answer ticks on the profile disappear. The permanent tool bar costs 83 pt.
  • Landscape shows the whole trip’s profile with every day end as a handle; in this direction it is the best surface for editing days.
