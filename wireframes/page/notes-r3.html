Strong

  • The most discoverable: the five common requests are visible on every screen without typing.
  • One olive line above the row names what it acts on (“For Day 4”, “For the whole trip”, “From here”) and follows the selection.
  • A tap leaves the box empty: the chips are the request, so the box never shows words you did not type.

Weak

  • The row, its caption and the chips are four layers above the content. While an answer shows, the phone map shrinks to a strip of about 150–180 pt.
  • The row is fixed: pharmacies, trains or “open on Sunday” still need typing, and the row does not teach how the box reads a sentence.
  • The row hides while the box holds typed text, so it moves in and out.
