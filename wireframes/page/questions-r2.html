Questions for you

The same questions are written out in the chat.

  1. Which way should the box show that it is more than a place search?R1: a standard field, and the answer’s chips are controls. R2: a scope chip inside the field (Whole trip, Day 4, Here) that follows your selection, and request starters when the empty box is focused. R3: a fixed row of five one-tap requests under the box. A mix is possible.Suggestion: R2.
  2. Are the picker rules right?A choice applies at once, so a picker has no Done button; a tap on the chip closes it. A removed filter stays as a struck chip, and a tap brings it back. After you edit a chip, the sentence you typed stays in the field in a faint colour, because the chips are now the request.Suggestion: yes to all three.
