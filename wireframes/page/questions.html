Questions for you

The same questions are written out in the chat.

  1. Which layout direction do you want for the planner?A Map first, B Profile first, C Query first, or D Map and strip with point or type. A mix is possible, for example D on the website and A on the iPhone.Suggestion: D on both, because the website and the app should be as similar as possible.
  2. On the iPhone, where does the box sit?At the top of the sheet, near the bottom of the screen and in thumb reach (as in Apple Maps), or at the top of the screen (as in Komoot and Google Maps).Suggestion: in the sheet, for one-handed use on the road.
  3. When you point at a day end on the strip, what does the box show?Option 1 (drawn in D): the box writes the request as words, “places to sleep, end of day 4”, with the same chips as a typed request. Option 2 (drawn in the query-box states): the box holds the place as a chip, “End of Day 4 · Valloire ×”, next to the cursor, and you can type the rest after it.Suggestion: option 2, because the box then never shows words you did not type.
  4. How free should the typed sentence be in v1?Level 2: any wording of one request, English and German first. Level 3: also combined requests, such as “campsites near a supermarket at the end of day 4”. Level 3 needs a model about four times larger and slower.Suggestion: Level 2, with a request language that can grow to Level 3 later.
  5. Shall I run the model experiment next?It needs about 3 to 5 days of agent work, about 2 GB of model downloads on your Mac, one session with your iPhone, and part of the test sentences from you.Suggestion: yes, as its own spike issue under #2236, after you pick the direction.
  6. Two quiet features of the strip: do you want them?First, keep a list answer on the strip as a layer (for example the water points of Day 4) until you remove it. Second, shade the stretch where a day can end after the riding time you choose (for example 5 to 6 h), shown only when you ask for places to sleep.Suggestion: yes to both.
  7. May I correct the date rule in #2236?“Shops open on Sunday” works without trip dates, because it filters on the Sunday hours. Only “shops open on Day 4” needs the date of Day 4.Suggestion: yes.
