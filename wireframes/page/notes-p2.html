P2 · Three views

  • A two-row navigation bar: Back, the plan name, search, Undo and “···”, then Map | Profile | Days. Each segment is one full-screen view.
  • At rest the box is docked at the bottom of the Map view, in thumb reach. An answer replaces the box in the same dock: chips, a count, the results, and one action while a row is selected.
  • Editing days: the clearest profile of the three (full width, low on the screen), and the days as one pure list.
  • Weak: the two-row bar costs 150 pt in every view, and the segments sit at the top, out of thumb reach. A list never shows next to the map, so you switch views more often. For a one-day route the segments add little.
