P1 · One sheet

  • The detent is the switch. Low: the box and one line. Medium: the profile, or the compact answer. High: the days, or the full answer. Focusing the box takes the sheet to the top and hides the map.
  • The one line under the box changes with the moment: the trip totals, the scrubber reading on the profile, or the answer count. So the low detent is always useful.
  • The picker opens under its chip and the sheet grows by exactly its height, so the list and the markers stay in view.
  • Weak: while an answer is open, the medium detent holds the answer, so the profile is one step away. On the profile only the selected day’s end is a drag handle; the other day ends are dots.
