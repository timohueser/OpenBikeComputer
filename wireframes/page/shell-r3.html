What went wrong, and the rules for this round

In the prototype, the phone showed the map, the box, the chips, the answer, the days and a large Send to device button at the same time. With the keyboard up, only a sliver of map and a sliver of answer were left. The profile had no place.

  • One job per state: the map, a list or the profile, and at most two of them together. Never the map, a list and the keyboard at once.
  • Search is its own state. When you focus the box, the search takes the full screen and the map hides. At rest the box stays in thumb reach.
  • An answer returns to the map. When the keyboard goes down, the map shows the results and a compact list; a selected result shows its one action.
  • No permanent primary button. “Save and send to device” is in the “···” menu; Send stays the main action of the route page after Done.
  • The profile has a home, in the app’s day colours, with a scrubber that shows its point on the map.
