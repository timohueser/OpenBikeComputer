Levels of detail

Each LOD is a zoom tier. LOD 0 is the coarsest (drawn when zoomed far out); the last LOD is the finest (full detail up close). A feature appears at its starting tier and every finer one. max m/px is the most zoomed-out scale a tier covers; simplify drops geometry detail finer than that many meters.

Features & styling

Toggle features to include and edit color (RGB565), z-index and stroke weight — these are global, shared across all LODs. The LODs column picks the coarsest tier a feature appears at (it also shows at every finer tier). Edits are saved automatically and persist between sessions.
