    Things to try

      New in this version

      • Your words stay in the field. While you type, the bar only marks what it understands. Enter, or the amber button in the field, sends every phrase to its slot.
      • A result carries the action. The first slot can say what to do with the place: find it, route to it, add it as a stop, or end the day at it. The top result then has the one amber button. Nothing changes before you press it, and “Undo” takes it back.
      • “Where” is on the profile. Drag across the profile for a stretch, tap a day for that day, or move the two handles. The places that match are dots on the profile, so you see what lies before a climb.
